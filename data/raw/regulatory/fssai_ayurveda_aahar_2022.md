# Food Safety and Standards (Ayurveda Aahar) Regulations, 2022

## Regulation 3: Ayurveda Aahar 
(1) "Ayurveda Aahar" means a food prepared in accordance with the recipes or ingredients and/or processes as per methods described in the authoritative books of Ayurveda listed under ‘Schedule A’ of these regulations.
(2) It does not include:
- Ayurvedic drugs or proprietary Ayurvedic medicines.
- Medicinal products, cosmetics, narcotic or psychotropic substances.
- Herbs listed under Schedule E of Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and Rules 1945.

## Regulation 4: General Requirements
(1) No person shall manufacture or sell Ayurveda Aahar intended for administration to infants up to the age of 24 months.
(2) Food Business Operator (FBO) shall formulate Ayurveda Aahar in accordance with the categories and requirements specified in Schedule A.
(3) The products shall not claim cure or mitigation of any specific disease, disorder, or condition (unless such claim is supported by Ayurvedic texts and approved).
(4) The label of Ayurveda Aahar shall bear the word "AYURVEDA AAHAR" on the principal display panel.

## Regulation 5: Claims
(1) The labelling, presentation and advertising shall not claim that the Ayurveda Aahar has the property of preventing, treating or curing a human disease or refer to such properties.
(2) Disease risk reduction claims are not permitted.
